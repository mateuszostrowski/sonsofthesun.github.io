About

SONS of the SUN combines the dirty sounds of 90's grunge, the high-energy attitude of punk rock and the heaviness of metal music to create their own unique take on alternative rock. In 2023 the band self-released their debut album REVELATIONS. Their sophomore release - DIVISIONS - is coming soon in 2026…

The band sticks to DIY ethics while recording their albums, shooting music videos and creating artwork. All music, words and images were created by human beings.

SONS of the SUN are not recommended for fans of polished, overproduced, AI-generated music.
